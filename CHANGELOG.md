# Changelog

Newest first. Dates are when the build was pushed.

## Nov 8 2026

- **Custom Cluster 0.1.24:** Sport, while BMW is guiding: the bottom strip becomes the route's progress, filled blue from its right end leftwards (distance travelled over travelled plus left; Guidance travelledDistance and finalDestinationInfo), with "18.4 km left" (the distance as BMW's navigation shows it) at the strip's left end and "0:24 left" (h:mm) at its right. Without a route it is the pedal strip again. The route messages are logged ("route destination raw", "route travelled raw") to confirm them on the first route. The bench drives a made-up 24 km route while GUIDING is on.
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.23:** the cluster follows route guidance instead of the drive mode: a new "While navigating" setting (Sport with the map by default; Regal, Off or no change) takes over while BMW is guiding (navigation.guiding.Guidance guidanceState), with the map window open whatever "Leave room for the map" says, and the theme comes back when the route ends. The drive mode settings (Change with SPORT / COMFORT / ECO PRO, In Comfort / Sport / Eco Pro) are gone, and so are the GT3 and M themes and their fonts; the theme is Regal by default (a saved GT3 or M choice falls back to it). With Custom HUD installed, the HUD now stays off while a cluster theme shows.
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.22:** a new Regal theme after a 1987 Buick Regal's dash, next to the others (Sport is unchanged). Chrome-framed side pods: a 12 V voltmeter left and coolant right, each needle pivoting from the pod's inner edge. Under them are the BRAKE lamp, the turn arrows (body.Light StatusFunctionsIndicators, blinking) and CRUISE (lit while BMW shows the ACC car). The centre panel has a speed needle pivoting on the frame line under the speed window, pointing at a straight row of mph numbers, 5 to 85 with 55 in amber at the middle, as the Turbo 3.8's; at a standstill the needle rests below the row, left of the 5. Two orange seven-segment VFD windows show the speed (in the units setting's unit), the odometer (MileageSupreme, taken as km), the gear row (P R N D, a gap, then the gear number; D lit in D and S, the number lit in M), the clock and the range. Below them are a fuel needle and an LED rev bar, yellow from the shift point. The song title sits centred on the bottom row. There is no map window. One shader plus text sheets: 5 layers, 3.3 MiB. The indicator and odometer raw values are logged ("indicators raw", "odometer raw") to confirm them on the first drive.
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.21:** Sport: the average consumption after the range on the bottom row (e.g. 9.8 l/100km), from the car's trip computer for the period picked on the center screen (this trip, since refuelling, individual, long term), in the car's own unit (l/100km, mpg, km/l). The raw values are logged ("consumption average raw", "consumption bc raw") to confirm the scaling on the first drive.
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.20:** Sport: the speed band in iDrive 8's colours: a cyan outer line, filled a deep cyan-blue brightening to cyan at the value; past the value both bands take the stock dark colours (navy for speed, dark maroon for rpm), sampled from a sharp photo of the car's own Sport view; the rpm fill pink-red; the outer lines about twice as thick as the inner ones; each bracket now runs outer line, a dark gap holding the redline, then the band against the inner line, as BMW's does, while up to the value the fill spans the whole width; the marks are short thick rounded dashes in the middle of the gap (the band narrowed to 32 px to make room); the fuel and oil gauges sit 27 px closer to the brackets.
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.19:** the tank is 59 L (a 2020 G20 M340i), not 65: the fuel gauge's marks read 59 / 30 / 0 L or 16 / 8 / 0 gal, and its fill is measured against 59 L.
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.18:** Sport: the fuel gauge's marks are the tank in litres or US gallons by the units setting (65 / 33 / 0 L, 17 / 9 / 0 gal, from config.tankLitres), with L or gal under the top mark, instead of 1 / 1/2 / 0.
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.17:** Sport: boost and the 12 V battery become two upright boxes left of the speed scale, mirroring the car-ahead box (boost yellow 0 to 1.5 bar, battery green by charge %, each value riding beside the top of its fill, the battery's in V). They are drawn in the main shader, so the layout uses 6 layers instead of 7.
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.16:** Sport: the speed and rpm numbers fade in as the value comes near, as iDrive 8 does: full white at the label, their own colour one label away (halfway between two labels both are half lit), instead of only the nearest one lighting up.
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.15:** Sport: the car ahead as a gauge right of the rpm scale instead of the GAP text row: an upright box filled from the bottom up to the gap (0 to 150 m) with the metres riding beside the top of the fill, the relative speed under it; the fill and numbers turn red while the car ahead closes faster than 10 km/h (config.acc.closingKmh). Nothing shows with no car ahead. A few text boxes no longer overlap their neighbours, so the layout packs into 7 layers and 6.1 MiB (was 8 and 10.4). The music title is centred under the pedal strip's middle.
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.14:** Sport: the fuel and oil gauges drawn as BMW's; a soft dark halo behind the gear keeps the map's lines from running through it.own Sport view draws them: flat ends, four segments, lit white on grey, the rail on the side away from the labels; oil's cold (blue) and hot (red) ends moved from the bar to its rail. The brackets' slanted outer lines no longer poke past the top and bottom lines at the corners, and each bracket has a single line along the top (the inner line's own top run is gone); the decorative gauge icon above the speed is gone; the fuel and oil gauges' bottom numbers line up with the bars' bottom ends; the speed and rev bands are empty (a faint tint) past the value, as BMW's.
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.13:** Sport: the song title BMW's player shows, in the middle of the bottom row, drawn at runtime in the car's own font so any script works (opentype.js plus a small rasterizer, no font shipped); cut with … when too long. The car's font paths are not known yet: until they go in config.music.fonts the app searches the usual folders, picks the fonts that cover the most scripts, and logs every font file it finds. The bench feeds demo titles in several scripts.
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.12:** Sport: the blue/red bottom strip is now the pedals, filled from its middle out: throttle to the left (last tenth red in kickdown), brake to the right; the boost and coolant text left it. Under the speed scale and below its bracket line, boost (0 to 1.5 bar, yellow) over the 12 V battery (green; bar = charge %, value = volts), each with its value at the end, replace the throttle and brake bars. Bottom row: clock and range at the left, coolant and outside temperature at the right, the middle kept free for a music title. The map-window mask now covers only the strip just above the fuel gauge, so BMW's three-digit speed shows (118 read as 18). The bench draws BMW's real speed.

## Nov 7 2026

- **Custom Cluster 0.1.11:** the Sport theme's map-window mask moved from under BMW's speed to the window's left edge, where BMW's speed-limit sign poked out above the fuel gauge; the line under BMW's speed shows again. The cluster bench draws that sign.
- **Custom HUD 0.1.5, Custom Cluster 0.1.10:** the car-ahead row reads "--" with no car instead of hiding: DataFrontVehicle's 4093..4095 and object ids 253..255 mean no object (with no car the car sends 4093 everywhere, which read as a real value). Its speed field is no longer used (following a car slowly it read 509): the relative speed comes from the gap's rate. The car-ahead log keeps each message's last value, so an unchanged message is not logged again.
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.9:** the Sport theme's throttle and brake bars are stacked and run to the bottom bar, the 12 V voltage and battery moved onto the bottom row after the remaining range, and BMW's line under its speed in the map window (whose break marks the speed limit) is kept black.
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.8:** the throttle bar's last segment turns red in kickdown (the OBD pedal at 87 % or more; 82 flat out, 91 in kickdown on the car).
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.7:** the throttle bar is empty at rest and full flat out: the OBD pedal reads 14.5 % at rest and 82 % flat out on the car (91 % in kickdown, which stays full).
- **Custom HUD 0.1.4, Custom Cluster 0.1.6:** the car ahead's gap is in 0.1 m steps (it read 10 times BMW's own), and its relative speed with it. The Sport throttle bar reads the engine unit's OBD pedal position (PID 0x49, 4 times a second): the car's AcceleratorPedal message jumped about during a slow press (its high byte looks like the message's alive counter). The OBD value is logged on change; config.pedal.obdRange maps its rest and flat-out readings to 0..100.
- **Custom HUD 0.1.3, Custom Cluster 0.1.5:** the car ahead's relative speed reads "--" while we stand still (the gap's rate is only noise then), and its row stays up as long as a car is measured. The throttle bar jumped about during a slow press on the car, so the accelerator pedal message is now logged on every change (up to 5 a second) with its other values, to find the right field and scaling. The layout check also warns about widgets over BMW's bottom-row gear letter, and the cluster bench draws BMW's gear (its gear box and that letter).
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.4:** the Sport theme shows the 12 V battery's charge in % after its voltage (vehicle.CDS statusBattery2; the raw value is logged on change to confirm the scaling on the car).
- **Custom HUD 0.1.2, Custom Cluster 0.1.3:** the clock follows the car's own time zone (summer time included): the apps' clock on the car is UTC, so it read 5 h ahead in Toronto; they now take the offset from the time the cluster shows (KombiIF statusShowTimeDate). config.utcOffsetMinutes still overrides it. The Sport theme's brake bar shows how hard the car slows while braking, on a log scale (0.1 g about 4 of 10 segments, 0.3 g 6, 0.6 g 8, 1 g full).
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.2:** under the Sport theme's left speed scale, a throttle bar (the accelerator pedal) and a brake bar (lit while the brake lights are on: the car gives no brake pressure), and under them the 12 V system voltage (OBD PID 0x42 from the engine unit, read every 2 s). The pedal's scaling is BMW's usual 0.025 %/bit, logged raw every 5 s to check against a full press. 48 V is not exposed by the car.
- **Custom Cluster 0.1.1:** the Sport theme's map window keeps a triangle in each corner black, where BMW's own Sport view showed through with its bracket ends, a gauge icon and hatched dial ends (the theme's own gauge icon there hides with the map window too), and the car ahead shows under the right rpm scale while one is measured: its gap and its speed against ours. The cluster bench draws what BMW shows in the map window: its speed and gear and those corner bits.
- **Custom HUD 0.1.1:** the car ahead shows in every mode while one is measured (cruise + nav: the gap only): its gap (m / ft) and its speed against ours (km/h / mph, minus = closing). An X3 sends only GapVehicleDrivingAhead, so the gap comes from that while it says a car is measured, and the relative speed from how fast the gap changes. The car-ahead log line is written only when the values change (the car repeats the same one every second). With cluster-render from james10424/idrive-apps-lib the HUD gives way to BMW's guidance for the whole route.
- **Custom HUD 0.1.0, Custom Cluster 0.1.0:** forked from bimmer-lab/apps (HUD Widgets 0.5.3, Cluster Themes 0.8.2) under new ids, so they install next to the originals. The HUD makes room for BMW's own content: it reads route guidance and the ACC display and switches between normal, cruise, nav and cruise + nav layouts (Daily has all four), and shows the gap and closing speed to the car ahead in cruise (raw until calibrated on the car). The cluster's Sport theme hides its own speed while the map window is open, so BMW's shows alone. The bench draws BMW's speed, speed-limit sign, ACC block and guidance, with GUIDING and ACC switches.
